# Withdraw stock — Public Web refusal

## Trigger

The caller requests withdrawal with a valid quantity and comment for a lot owned by a different account.

## Outcome

The operation stops at the adjustment check before invoking the helper or reaching the normal stock flush. No Amount, Unknown amount or Last stocktake change is accepted.

## Edge cases

- A closed operation decision also stops helper execution. Refusal does not promise one response envelope across anonymous and authenticated requests.
